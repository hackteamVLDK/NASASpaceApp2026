# Review of the playable campaign: animation and logic (2026-11-14)

How it was checked: the game was played in a real browser (Edge, 1920x1080) with scripted runs and
screenshots at the interesting moments (walking, building, night, storm, trips, tutorial, decisions), and
the engine was fuzzed with random commands (80 seeds x 35 Sols) against a list of rules that must always
hold. [SCENARIO.md](../SCENARIO.md) stayed the reference; it was not edited.

The fuzz now lives in `tests/campaign-fuzz.test.mjs` (1.6 s). The fixes below each have a test in
`tests/review-fixes.test.mjs` or `tests/movers.test.mjs`; both fail on the code as it was before.

## Animation and screen problems found, and fixed

| # | Problem (what the player saw) | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The surface was never visible: no solar panels, airlock, lander, antenna, sky, day and night. The Commander "outside" simply vanished. | The camera framing put the ground line under the 98 px top bar. SCENARIO 1 asks for a visible surface strip. | `GAMEPLAY_VIEW` is now zoom 0.46, centre 1690: the whole base fits and about 147 px of surface show under the top bar. `tests/flow.test.mjs` checks it. The soil and sky were widened so no dark bands appear at the screen edges. |
| A2 | The Commander walked diagonally through floors and walls. | A straight line between two places. | `src/render/movers.js`: sideways to the shaft, up or down the shaft (climbing frames), then into the room. Unit-tested for every pair of places. |
| A3 | Drones teleported to the room they build. | No movement at all. | Drones use the same movers: rolling frames when going to a place, hovering up the shaft, carrying a girder to a build site. |
| A4 | Builder drones switched to the "powered down for the dark" frames at night, while the build bar kept moving. | Frame choice by clock only. SCENARIO 3.10: drones never sleep. | Working drones weld day and night. Only idle drones power down at night. |
| A5 | The EVA suit appeared while the Commander was still inside the base. | The suit was chosen from the destination. | The suit follows the body: indoor sprite in the base, EVA on the surface. |
| A6 | The lander disappeared during every expedition; the Rover was never drawn; the Commander vanished instantly on launch and reappeared on return. | `!s.away` hid the lander; the Rover sprite existed but was not used. | The lander stays. The Rover parks in the Garage, the Commander walks to it and boards, it drives out of the right edge, and later rolls back in. The Commander steps off and walks to his place. |
| A7 | Drones #02 and #03 stood in the Garage although the cutscene buries them in the mounds on the surface. | Gameplay ignored the mounds. | They wait in the mounds (blinking red LEDs); a kit digs them out (the "shaking the sand off" frames), then they roll down the shaft. |
| A8 | The Base tab showed "upgrading 0%" and the Workshop tab "Crafting 0%" until the job ended. | The tab was repainted only when the whole-number state changed, and upgrade and craft progress were not part of it. | They are part of the signature now, so the percentage moves. |
| A9 | The Rover radio bar ran backwards after a radio decision that makes the trip longer. | Progress was `(now - start) / (end - start)` with a moving end. | `awayProgress()` keeps the bar monotone (also used by the Threats strip and HP drain). |
| A10 | After every decision card the game stayed paused until the player pressed 1x. | The pause was never undone. | The speed before the card comes back when the decision is made. A card also no longer paints over the loot screen. |
| A11 | The right column ran off the screen while the Rover radio was showing. | Fixed heights. | The tab list scrolls; the column always fits. |
| A12 | Day, storm and flare had little to see. | (hidden surface) | Twinkling stars at night, dust streaks in a storm, a green sky tint while a flare card is open. |

## Logic problems found, and fixed

| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | A drone could hold 3 or more waiting jobs when the player picked that drone (the cap was checked only when every drone was full). | `queueCheck` takes the chosen drone and refuses a third job. |
| L2 | Random events started while the Commander was on an expedition (34 times in 80 runs). SCENARIO 2.1, Base Autopilot: they wait until he is home. | The event timer re-arms every 30 minutes while he is away, or until 90 minutes after the loot screen. |
| L3 | The Tasks list could show the same mini-game twice (Dilemma 4 B plus the black box both add "antenna"). | Every push goes through `addTask`. |
| L4 | 0 Water did not pause the Greenhouse, and 0 Water or 0 Food did not cost Morale (SCENARIO 3.1, "At 0"). | Greenhouse output stops at 0 Water; Morale -5 per Sol at 0 Water and at 0 Food. |
| L5 | The Lab's Research output (+5 / +8 per Sol) was dropped, so Research never grew. | Research is accumulated. Nothing spends it yet (open item P5 below). |
| L6 | After a trip the state kept `where = 'away'` for one tick, so the Commander was invisible behind the loot card. | The location is refreshed the moment the Rover is home. |
| L7 | An order or `surface` command moved the Commander only at the next tick, so nothing happened while the game was paused. | The location is refreshed at once. |
| L8 | A rescue (HP 0 on a trip) ended in Med-Rest but was not counted, so the "Well Rested" goal and the report were wrong. | `stats.medRests` is incremented. |
| L9 | `astra.rad.partial_room` was never said; `flags.flare` was never set, so the flare tint and the x3 radiation were dead code. | ASTRA warns once on the first visit to a right-wing room without the Roof Layer. The flare flag is set while a flare card (E1, D5) is open. |
| L10 | A drone ordered to upgrade a room that was still being built was told "Build it first." | "That room is still being built." |

## Still open (not fixed here)

- Plan items P2 (breakdowns), P3 (Bumpy and Rough consequences: 0.5 Sol late, extra wheel repair), P4 (Ice Run mode),
  P5 (Research gating of the Deep Scan: Research now grows, nothing consumes it), P6 (Recreation refill),
  P7 (Earth windows every 3 Sols, bent dish), P10, P11, P13: see [GAMEPLAY_PLAN.md](GAMEPLAY_PLAN.md), step 4.
- Fitness, Sol Plans and the Fitness factor of the expedition HP formula (SCENARIO 3.10, 3.11) are not modelled.
- Mini-game failures are simplified compared with SCENARIO 4.x (for example Heat Pump: -10 Heat per failure, not
  "each unconnected room -10"). The Wheel Vacuum "failed prep raises the risk one step" cannot happen, because the
  free retry and the drone help after three failures always end in a win.
- Resting in the Rover on the 2-Sol trip (+6 HP) is not modelled.
- Art: the climbing sprites bring their own ladder, drawn over the shaft's ladder. Drone #02 and #03 portraits and the
  crew heads wait for the character prompts.
- The gameplay zoom is smaller than before (0.54 to 0.46) so the surface fits. If it feels small, tune `GAMEPLAY_VIEW`
  in `src/flow/intro.js`; the flow test says how much surface must stay visible.
