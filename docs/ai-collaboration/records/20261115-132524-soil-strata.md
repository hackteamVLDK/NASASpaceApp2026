## Goal
Calm, coherent pixel-art strata in the game's palette:
- Continuous layers: the boundaries are smooth, gently wavy lines across the whole width (deterministic noise from the
  existing `hash`), no seams, no staircase. Colours sampled from the current terrain tiles (keep the palette: rust/ochre
  near the top, browner, then greyer basalt with depth). Subtle pixel dithering at the boundaries is fine.
- The tilted delta (a real feature under Jezero) as ONE soft band of a few gently inclined layers in a limited region,
  fading out at its edges, not a repeating diagonal pattern.

- Rocks: crisp pixel shapes (integer pixels at the soil's 0.5 scale, a 1-px darker outline, one highlight), no blur,
